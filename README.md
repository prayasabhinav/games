On one screen or each on your own phone. No sign-in. Each game also has a page with its rules and print files: [3 Quarters](https://prayasabhinav.github.io/games/3-quarters/), [All or Nothing](https://prayasabhinav.github.io/games/all-or-nothing/) and [Matbhed](https://prayasabhinav.github.io/games/matbhed/).

Games I designed, with their rules and the files to print and play them.

- **3 Quarters** (2022): a board game I made as a warm-up for students. Players place, turn and remove quarter-circle pieces on a printed board to complete three-quarter circles, and score the points on the pieces. The first to 25 wins.
- **All or Nothing** (2020): a card game for 2 to 4 players about completing a 12-slice pizza. Players add and take away slices with number cards, and whoever completes the pizza scores a point for every card in the centre.
- **Matbhed** (2023): a board game I designed with Jaskaran Singh for Conflictorium's Re-Art25 project, about Article 25 of the Constitution of India, the freedom of religion. Players cross a maze and answer cards on religion and the law, printed in English and Gujarati, and a Judge scores each answer. Its files and rules are in [`matbhed/`](matbhed/README.md).

3 Quarters and All or Nothing are among the known games in boardgameBuilder, at [boardgame.syntax.prayasabhinav.net](https://boardgame.syntax.prayasabhinav.net), where each can be downloaded as a design and opened to change.

I first published those two in 2022 under another GitHub account of mine, which I can no longer sign in to. This repository is their home now. The texts are as I wrote them then; only their links to images and print files now point to the copies here.

## Play in a browser

Each game can also be played in a browser, free and with no sign-in, on one screen or on separate devices: [3 Quarters](https://prayasabhinav.github.io/games/3-quarters/play/), [All or Nothing](https://prayasabhinav.github.io/games/all-or-nothing/play/) and [Matbhed](https://prayasabhinav.github.io/games/matbhed/play/). For separate devices, one player hosts and the others join with a link or a five-letter code; moves pass through a small relay, games-relay.prayasabhinav.net, which keeps nothing. In 3 Quarters and All or Nothing a seat can be played by the computer. When a game ends, each page offers a short form to say how it went. Each page lists, under *How this version reads the rules*, where the written rules left something open and what the page does there.
