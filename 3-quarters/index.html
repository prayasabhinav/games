3 Quarters

A board game of quarter circles, made in 2022 as a warm-up for students. Free to print and change.

Play

In your browser, on one screen or each on your own phone. No sign-in.

How to play

Players
2 to 4 (the pieces are printed for four)
To win
25 points
You need
the board, eight pieces for each player, a four-sided die, a coin, and a phone to time each move

Set up. Play on the red rows with 2 players, add the green rows for 3, and the blue row for 4. Each player takes their eight quarter-circle pieces. Toss a coin to see who starts.

Pieces. A piece fills one square and faces one of its four corners. Its pattern sets its points: crosshatched 1, dotted 2, plain 3, black 4.

A turn. Roll the die first. Then arrange one piece: add one of yours to an empty square, turn any piece on the board, yours or another player's, or remove one. A piece you add or turn goes round as many corners as the die shows; a 4 turns it full circle.

Three quarters. Three pieces in three of the four squares around one shared corner, each facing that corner, make a 3/4 circle. All three must have the same pattern. They can belong to anyone.

Remove. To remove a piece, place a piece with the same pattern on it and take both, without adding their points. A player can remove a piece only three times in a game.

Score. The player who completes the 3/4 circle takes its pieces off the board and scores their points. If one of them is on a yellow square, the score is doubled.

Keep the board. The player whose move empties the board loses 10 points.

Time. Each move has to be made in 20 seconds.

Win. The first player to 25 points wins.
