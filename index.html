Games by Prayas Abhinav

Games I designed, from 2011 to 2023. Each one plays in your browser, on one screen or each on your own phone, with no sign-in. Each has a page with its rules and the files to print and play it.

The Decay of Meaning 2011

Two avatars of me chase each other round a planet, and each word one eats changes its speed. Shown at Words: a user's manual, Exhibit 320, New Delhi, and kept as the 2011 game.

▶ PlayThe game

Blackpool 2019

Two to four players race three travellers each out of a whirlpool whose rings pull them back. Made with Priyamvada Gaur.

▶ PlayThe game

All or Nothing 2020

A card game for 2 to 4 players about completing a 12-slice pizza, adding and taking away slices with number cards.

▶ PlayThe game

3 Quarters 2022

A board game I made as a warm-up for students: place, turn and remove quarter-circle pieces to complete three-quarter circles.

▶ PlayThe game

Matbhed 2023

A board game about Article 25 of the Constitution of India, the freedom of religion, made with Jaskaran Singh for Conflictorium. Cards in English and Gujarati.
